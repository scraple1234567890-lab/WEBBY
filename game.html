How Focus and Types work

Focus

  • Focus is your resource (0 to 6). You spend it on bigger actions.
  • Attack always gives +1 Focus (and deals Sight damage).
  • Guard gives +1 Focus and makes the next hit deal 50% less.
  • Wind costs 2 Focus. Fire costs 3 Focus.
  • Heal costs 1 Focus (or 2 if you are Bound). You have limited charges.
  • If you are Bound (Touch effect), your magic costs +1 Focus until it wears off. Guard breaks Bind.

Types

Damage is calculated like this:

Damage = Base × STAB × Effectiveness (rounded)

  • Move type: each action is a type (Wind / Fire / Sight / Earth / Touch).
  • STAB (Same Type Attack Bonus): if the move matches one of your types, it gets ×1.2.
  • Effectiveness: some types hit harder or softer into other types.
  • Dual types multiply together (example: Wind vs Fire+Sight uses both matchups).
Quick hints:
Wind▶Fire• Fire▶Earth• Earth▶Wind
Touch▶Sight• Sight▶Fire

Tip: the move buttons show the multiplier (x1.44, x0.81, etc.) so you can plan before committing.
