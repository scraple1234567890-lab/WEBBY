Character dossier Profile

Magic profile:

  • Alignment: Light
  • Element: None
  • School: Sound
  • Inclination: Resonance Mapping (vibration sense + dampening)

Who Devante is: Devante is the kind of calm that makes chaos feel embarrassed. He speaks in short sentences, listens in long ones, and carries a tuning-fork charm because silence can have teeth in the wrong places. When the city gets loud, he gets quieter, and that is when you should pay attention.

Notable tells & facts:

  • Hums without noticing when he is thinking; the pitch shifts with his mood.
  • Loud chaos triggers migraines, so he fights "clean" and prefers precision over volume.
  • Protective of younger students and always seems to arrive where help is needed before anyone asks.
  • Has history with the Arena and is quietly being watched for Holy Night potential.

Signature techniques:

  • Echo Map: a low hum that returns as a mental sketch of rooms, hollows, and hidden doors.
  • Quiet Halo: dampens sound in a small radius to calm panic or hide conversation.
  • Glassbreak Note: a precise pitch that fractures brittle materials (rarely used, hard to control).

Relationships:

  • Belle: he teaches her breathing rhythm; she jokes he is a human metronome. (search)
  • Axel: mutual respect from a safe distance: the runner and the shield. (dossier)
  • Pandora: something about her timing rings too perfect, like a rehearsed note. (search)
