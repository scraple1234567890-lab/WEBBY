Axel

Steel-nerved and quick to read a room.

← Back to search Roster
Character dossier Book 2 profile

Magic profile:

  • Alignment: Neutral
  • Element: None
  • School: Touch
  • Inclination: Friction Weave (grip / slide control)

Who Axel is: Axel moves like the city is a puzzle only he can solve, curb to roof, shadow to streetlamp. He smiles easy, listens harder, and keeps favors in his pocket the way others keep coins. When things turn ugly, he is usually the first person to find an exit and the last person to leave someone behind.

Notable tells & facts:

  • Never repeats the same route twice in a week (pattern recognition is how the city eats you).
  • Panic makes his power overcorrect: too much grip, not enough release.
  • Two pairs of gloves: one for climbing, one for shaking hands.
  • Has ties to the Black Market and the Illegal District (by necessity, not loyalty).

Signature techniques:

  • Grip Bloom: blooms friction under his palm, turning slick surfaces into a stable hold.
  • Slide Lace: thins friction underfoot for silent escapes and impossible landings.
  • Stillpoint Tap: a quick touch that kills momentum for a heartbeat (useful, dangerous, exhausting).

Relationships:

  • Elroy: the one person Axel trusts to "read him back." (search)
  • Belle: fire on a leash, courage in a hoodie. (search)
  • Alethea: her replacement glasses feel like a warning; he pretends he is not curious. (dossier)

Major scenes:

  • Threads in the black market
  • Police pressure

Haunts:

  • Gutterglass
  • Black Market
  • Illegal District
