Belle

Sound-led, quick-smiling, always listening for exits.

← Back to search Roster
Character dossier Book 2 profile

Magic profile:

  • Alignment: Neutral
  • Magic types: Sound + Fire
  • Inclination: —

Who Belle is: Belle moves like the city is a song only she can hear in full. She reads crowds by cadence, alleyways by echo, and she keeps an exit tucked behind the next beat. When she’s cornered, her Sound magic sharpens into heat: a Fire-thread that snaps like a matchstrike and clears just enough space to vanish.

Notable tells & facts:

  • Never repeats the same route twice in a week (pattern recognition is how the city eats you).
  • Panic makes her power overcorrect: too much grip, not enough release.
  • Two pairs of gloves: one for climbing, one for shaking hands.
  • Has ties to the Black Market and the Illegal District (by necessity, not loyalty).

Signature techniques:

  • Angle-Read: tracks motion lines and micro-shifts to predict where a strike will land a heartbeat early.
  • Blindspot Step: slips through the narrow slice of attention no one is guarding.
  • Exit Mark: tags a route in her mind so she can re-find it even in smoke, panic, or darkness.

Relationships:

  • Elroy: the one person Belle trusts to "read her back." (dossier)
  • Alethea: her replacement glasses feel like a warning; Belle pretends she is not curious. (dossier)

Major scenes:

  • Threads in the black market
  • Police pressure

Haunts:
