Relen

A prodigy under too much light.

← Back to search Roster
Character dossier Headmaster

Magic profile:

  • Alignment: Light
  • Element: Water
  • School: Sight
  • Inclination: Hardlight Construction (solid light constructs)

Who Relen is: The city calls him a miracle and means it as both compliment and warning. Relen became Headmaster of the School of Sight at twenty-four, young enough to still be learning the taste of his own name, old enough to know how quickly a title can become a leash. He fights like a mathematician: clean angles, closed loops, and no wasted motion. Where others throw light to blind, Relen builds it into something that holds.

Notable tells & facts:

  • Publicly polite, privately exhausting: every room expects him to prove he belongs in it.
  • His constructs are brightest when he is calm; anger makes them flare, beautiful and brittle.
  • He prefers restraint over injury, which makes him terrifying in the hands of anyone with bad intentions.
  • The faculty is split on him: half see the future, half see a convenient pawn.

Signature techniques:

  • Prism Manacles: hardlight chains that snap into place around wrists, ankles, or weapons.
  • Lumen Lattice: a web of linked chains that can block a corridor, catch a falling ally, or trap a rushing foe.
  • Chainline Pull: anchors a chain to a surface and yanks himself or an opponent across a space in a blink.
  • Gleam Ward: a thin shield of layered light that turns strikes aside like sliding glass.

Relationships:

  • Alethea: he watches her potential like it is a lit fuse, hoping it becomes a lantern instead. (dossier)
  • Devante: one of the few people whose calm feels real to him. (dossier)
  • Pandora: her “help” is always a fraction too perfect, like a reflection that learned to smile. (search)
